The Thesis

Standard academic wikis often languish in private channels, and formal symposiums lack room for unvarnished, practical problem-solving. Bioinforum is built to dissect preprints, benchmark pipelines, and debug Nextflow, R, and Python workflows—without the institutional posturing.
