// The Thesis

Science in the Trenches

Standard academic wikis often stagnate behind institutional logins, and traditional symposiums leave little room for raw, practical problem-solving. Bioinforum was built as an independent computational salon: dissecting preprints, benchmarking pipelines, and troubleshooting sequencing workflows without bureaucratic overhead.
